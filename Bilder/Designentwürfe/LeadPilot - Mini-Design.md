# Lead Pilot Mini Design

A compact mini design direction for Lead Pilot: dark, sharp, modern, with cyan as the main signal and orange as the accent.

## Visual direction

- Background: deep black / charcoal.
- Primary: cyan.
- Accent: orange.
- Support: white and soft gray.
- Style: minimal, energetic, slightly futuristic.

## Look and feel

- Clean surfaces.
- Rounded buttons and pills.
- Thin borders.
- Soft glow on active elements.
- Strong contrast.
- Very little decoration.

## Suggested palette

- `bg` `#14161A`
- `surface` `#1B1F24`
- `text` `#FFFFFF`
- `muted` `#A7B0BA`
- `cyan` `#00D9C6`
- `orange` `#FF7A3D`
- `border` `#2A3038`

## Mini layout concept

### Top bar
- Logo on the left.
- One nav item or none.
- CTA button on the right.

### Hero
- Big headline.
- Short subline.
- One cyan primary button.
- One orange secondary highlight or badge.

### Feature cards
- 2 or 3 cards max.
- Dark surfaces.
- Cyan icons or markers.
- Orange only for one standout card or highlight.

### Footer strip
- Small muted text.
- No heavy borders.
- Very calm and minimal.

## Component style

### Button
- Pill shape.
- Cyan fill for primary.
- Orange fill only for special CTA.
- No overly large shadows.

### Card
- Dark surface.
- Border in subtle gray.
- Radius medium to large.
- Compact content.

### Badge
- Small pill.
- Cyan outline or fill.
- Used for labels like "New" or "Live".

## One-screen example

**Headline:** Lead Pilot drives leads forward.

**Subline:** A compact system for modern sales workflows.

**Primary CTA:** Get started

**Accent:** New feature

## Design rule

If you need to choose between adding more elements or making the screen cleaner, make it cleaner.

## Best use cases

- Landing page hero.
- Simple dashboard.
- Product intro section.
- Brand overview slide.
